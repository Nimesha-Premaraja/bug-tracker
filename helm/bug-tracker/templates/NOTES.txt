Thank you for installing the bug-tracker chart.

1. Get the application URL by running these commands:

  export POD_NAME=$(kubectl get pods -l app={{ include "bug-tracker.fullname" . }} -o jsonpath="{.items[0].metadata.name}")
  kubectl port-forward $POD_NAME 5000:5000

Then open http://127.0.0.1:5000

2. Secrets
  - SECRET_KEY and DATABASE_URL are stored in a Kubernetes Secret named: {{ include "bug-tracker.fullname" . }}-secret
